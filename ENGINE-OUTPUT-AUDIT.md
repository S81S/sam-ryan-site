# Engine output audit — 2026-10-11

Ratings are display information, separate from VIN installation evidence. Exact year, model, output version, and where necessary trim, transmission and fuel must match.

The present engine-card catalog has 91 entries. 90 now have sourced power information, including ranges for broad older-engine preferences and one explicitly labeled development target. This is not 90 distinct engines or complete coverage of every catalog model and historical used vehicle.

## Remaining evidence gaps

- 2027 Ram Chassis Cab 6.4L / 8HP75: the verified 375 hp / 429 lb-ft announcement explicitly names the HD transmission. Do not inherit it for 8HP75 without confirmation.
- 2027 Cherokee Trailhawk: official release confirms 210 hp total. The reviewed source does not publish torque; do not copy 2026 torque without a 2027 source.
- Pacifica plug-in hybrid: 260 hp combined; no combined torque is published in the reviewed factory guide. Do not substitute gas-engine torque.
- 2027 Charger Super Bee: 600 hp is a development target, not a final certified production rating. Torque remains unpublished on the reviewed page.
- Recon: current retail capability page says 670 hp / 620 lb-ft; older launch material says 650 hp / 620 lb-ft. Both sources are retained in the output registry. The older trim-data record still needs a source conflict update.
- Model entries without an engine decision (including some announced 2027 models) and additional used-vehicle model years need separate coverage; do not treat this card audit as their completion.
