Car-shopping tips

Know what to ask.
Feel ready to choose.

Use this checklist to narrow your options, compare two vehicles and make the most of your visit. Sam and Ryan can help with the details on your shortlist.

Start with your everyday needs

How many people ride with you? What do you carry? Which features would you use every day? Write down your must-haves and separate them from the extras you would simply enjoy.

Compare the full picture

Look at the asking price alongside mileage, condition, service history and equipment. Ask for the total price including taxes and applicable fees, so you can compare the same information for each vehicle.

Check the actual vehicle’s equipment

Trim names are a starting point. Use the original window sticker to review factory packages, then ask us to confirm the features on the vehicle today. An original sticker does not describe current condition or later modifications. Open the comparison tool →

Bring your questions to the test drive

Check seat comfort, visibility, passenger space and cargo room. Try the controls you use most. Ask before bringing items to test for fit, and confirm that your chosen vehicle is available before you travel.

Ask about a used vehicle’s history

Review the available vehicle-history report and ask about service records, keys, known issues and inspection options. Ask what warranty coverage, if any, applies to that specific vehicle.

Still choosing between two?

Send us the stock numbers and tell us what matters most to you. We can help you work through the differences. Ask Sam or Ryan →

Shopping for a HEMI and cooled seats?

Ask for the exact engine and seat equipment instead of relying on a trim name. Our search checks the original VIN-matched sticker and shows the factory wording. Heated seats alone do not establish cooled seats, and cabin air conditioning does not establish seat ventilation.

For a sunroof requirement, choose either “with sunroof” or “no sunroof.” We leave omitted features unconfirmed unless complete sticker evidence and reviewed factory rules establish whether they are included.
