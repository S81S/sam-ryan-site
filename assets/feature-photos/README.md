# Verified vehicle feature photos

These are unmodified dealer photographs used by the Perfect Match photo guide.
Each photo's original source URL, gallery position, vehicle VIN, stock number,
model year, and verified feature evidence are recorded in
`data/feature-photo-guide.json` at the repository root.

Photos illustrate the specific vehicle and equipment identified in each card.
Keep original content and disclose relevant photo limitations in the catalog.
Do not replace these photos with generated depictions or infer equipment solely
from appearance. Trim availability and replacement rules live separately in
`data/photo-trim-path.json`.
