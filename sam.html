Meet Sam Sweitzer.

Sam created and owns Cars With Sam to make it easier to find the vehicle features that matter to you, compare the equipment on your shortlist and work directly with a real person through the purchase.

He is a sales consultant at Covert Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram of Austin, 8107 Research Blvd, Austin, Texas. You can find Samuel Sweitzer in Covert’s official staff directory. Teammate Ryan Sugrue also helps shoppers through the site.

Start with Find Your Car for a feature-based search, use Compare for exact VINs or stock numbers, or explore the buying guides. Sam can help check your must-haves, arrange a walkaround and work through the details with you.
