Jeep Compass: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Jeep Compass, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Does every Compass have four-wheel drive?

The U.S. 2026 Compass has standard Jeep Active Drive 4x4. Older model years and vehicles from other markets can differ, so check the actual drivetrain when comparing a used Compass.

What does Trailhawk add that matters off-road?

The 2026 Trailhawk adds Active Drive Low, Rock mode and additional ground clearance, along with all-terrain tires. Those features serve rougher routes; choose them for your intended terrain rather than the badge alone.

Does the turbo Compass need premium fuel?

Jeep recommends regular unleaded for the U.S. 2026 Compass. Its 2.0-liter turbo engine produces 200 horsepower and 221 pound-feet of torque; check older vehicles separately because their engines can differ.

Jeep Compass FAQ ↗

Can a Compass tow a small camper or trailer?

The U.S. 2026 Compass has a maximum tow rating of 2,000 pounds. Use the trailer’s loaded weight, then check required equipment and the vehicle’s capacity before deciding whether it fits your plans.

Jeep Compass capability ↗

How much room is there behind the rear seats?

The 2026 Compass has 27.2 cubic feet behind the rear seats and 59.8 with them folded. Bring your stroller or regularly carried gear to test the opening and usable shape.

Jeep Compass FAQ ↗

Does it come with a spare tire?

Jeep lists a full-size spare for the 2026 Compass Trailhawk and a tire service kit for other models. Check under the cargo floor, especially on a used vehicle where equipment may have changed.

Jeep Compass FAQ ↗

Can I get the larger screen and wireless CarPlay?

The 2026 Compass offers a 10.1-inch touchscreen with Uconnect 5 and wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto support. Screen and navigation equipment should be compared on the actual vehicles, especially across model years.

Jeep Compass technology ↗

Which Compass features help in stop-and-go traffic?

Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop and Go is listed as standard on 2026 Limited, Limited Altitude and Trailhawk. It adjusts speed for traffic ahead; the driver remains responsible for attention and control.

Jeep Compass technology ↗

Can I get heated seats and a hands-free liftgate?

The 2026 Compass offers heated front seats and a heated steering wheel. A foot-activated power liftgate is listed on Limited, Limited Altitude and Trailhawk; compare the exact year and installed equipment.

How do I judge the reliability of a used Compass?

Evaluate its model year, maintenance and inspection results rather than relying on a single owner story. Run its VIN through NHTSA for unrepaired safety recalls and ask for records of completed repairs.
