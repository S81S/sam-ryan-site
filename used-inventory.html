Compare the actual vehicles

Two vehicles with the same model and trim can have different equipment. Open a vehicle’s details to see photos, stock number, VIN and the listed price, then add the vehicles you like to Compare. Where a readable original window sticker is available and matched to the VIN, we use it to help check factory equipment.

A window sticker describes the vehicle as built. It does not establish its current condition, service history or whether equipment has been changed. Missing or unreadable sticker information stays unconfirmed. See how window-sticker comparisons work.

Before you visit

Send Sam or Ryan the stock number or VIN and ask for current availability, mileage, the total purchase price including taxes and fees, and any questions about vehicle history or condition. Listing prices and availability can change. We can help confirm the details and arrange a closer look.

Can I compare a used vehicle with a new one?

Yes. Select Both in Find Your Car, choose your vehicles and open Compare. Equipment that cannot be confirmed is identified so you can ask about it before deciding.
