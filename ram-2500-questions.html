Ram 2500: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Ram 2500, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Do I need a Ram 2500, or is a 1500 enough?

Bring loaded trailer weight, tongue weight, passenger count and cargo weight. Compare those against each exact truck’s ratings. Moving to a 2500 can help, but the badge alone does not establish sufficient payload.

Should I buy the 6.4-liter gas engine or Cummins diesel?

The 2026 diesel offers more maximum towing capability, while the gas lineup has a higher maximum payload figure. Compare your actual configurations and typical workload; the highest towing and payload numbers describe different trucks.

Why can a diesel have less payload than a gas truck?

Payload is the remaining capacity within the truck’s weight limits. A heavier engine and extra equipment use part of it. Check the actual payload label rather than substituting a brochure maximum from another configuration.

Is a Power Wagon the best choice for towing a big camper?

Power Wagon’s off-road setup has its own ratings, substantially different from some other 2500 configurations. Compare the trailer’s loaded hitch weight with the specific truck’s payload before choosing its trail hardware for a towing job.

Does fifth-wheel prep mean the hitch is already included?

The available prep package provides integrated mounting provisions. The correct hitch is still a separate item to confirm, together with trailer clearance, loaded pin weight and the truck’s applicable fifth-wheel or gooseneck rating.

What changes when I compare an older diesel with a 2026?

The 2026 Cummins pairs with an eight-speed heavy-duty automatic. Do not apply its specifications to older trucks with different engines or transmissions. Compare model-year documentation, service records and the actual equipment on each candidate.

Ram 2500 capability ↗

Which cameras will help me hitch and back up my trailer?

A backup camera, surround-view system and trailer camera functions are different capabilities. Compare the installed camera package and required trailer accessories, then demonstrate the views you will use with your particular trailer.

Ram 2500 features ↗

Can this truck run my equipment from its factory outlets?

Some 2026 heavy-duty configurations offer onboard power up to 2.4 kilowatts. Confirm the installed inverter, outlet locations and operating limits. A standard cabin outlet should not be assumed to provide that higher output.

Does the diesel suit lots of short trips and idling?

Read the diesel’s exhaust-filter, DEF and maintenance instructions alongside your daily use. Ask how regeneration is handled and inspect service history on a used truck; engine preference alone does not answer ownership fit.

Does rear air suspension increase how much I can carry?

Air leveling can help manage ride height, but it does not replace the truck’s weight ratings. Keep the load within its payload, axle, tire and hitch limits even when the truck appears level.
