2026 Chrysler Pacifica Trims Explained: Select, Limited & Pinnacle

Start with how you carry people and cargo. Select, Limited and Pinnacle do not use the same second-row arrangement, and the highest trim is not automatically the best fit for frequent floor-stowing. This page covers the U.S. 2026 gasoline Pacifica, with standard FWD configurations.

A practical way to narrow the choice

  1. Begin with the second row

    Select and Limited list second-row Stow ’n Go floor-stowing seats. Pinnacle uses easy-slide captain chairs that do not stow into the floor. Try the operation before choosing.

  2. Choose the cabin finish you prefer

    The guide records leatherette for Select, Nappa leather for Limited and quilted Nappa leather for Pinnacle. Compare the actual seating surfaces, adjustment and ventilation rather than choosing only from trim names.

  3. Separate included family equipment from packages

    Pinnacle includes the recorded surround-view camera, rear entertainment and FamCAM equipment. Limited lists those features through the applicable groups. An equipped Limited can therefore differ from its base trim specification.

Questions that change the buying decision

Do all Pacifica seats fold into the floor?

No. In this 2026 gas comparison, Select and Limited have second-row Stow ’n Go; Pinnacle’s second-row captain chairs do not floor-stow. Check the third-row operation separately. These facts should not be transferred to the plug-in hybrid.

Do I need Pinnacle for a 360-degree camera?

Not necessarily. The factory guide lists the Safety Sphere Group as an option for Select and Limited, and surround view as included on Pinnacle. Check the exact vehicle’s sticker and ask for a demonstration of its camera views.

Is all-wheel drive standard on these trims?

The comparison uses standard FWD configurations. AWD is separately available on Select, Limited and Pinnacle. Verify the drivetrain on the vehicle you are considering.

Is a heated seat the same as a ventilated seat?

No. Heating and ventilation are separate features. The guide records ventilated front seats on Limited and Pinnacle; do not infer ventilation from a heated-seat control or a leather seating surface.

What should I try with my family before deciding?

Bring the stroller, luggage or other items you regularly carry. Check second-row operation, third-row access and remaining cargo space with the needed seats in use. Follow the owner’s manual for seating and child-restraint installation.

Ask for a demonstration of your must-haves

Ask for a side-by-side seat-folding demonstration, the camera views and the family entertainment controls. Include your passenger and cargo needs so the demonstration answers your actual question.
