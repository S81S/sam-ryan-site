Factory stickers and WindowSticker.org

Where available, we link to a manufacturer’s original window sticker and check its VIN against the vehicle. Some external sticker lookups use WindowSticker.org. When that service supplies the document, the lookup identifies it with source credit. A link to a manufacturer’s document is identified by that document’s source; we do not label every sticker as coming from the same provider.

WindowSticker.org’s coverage information explains why some makes or model years return specifications instead of an original label. Availability varies. Provider credit identifies the source and does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

VINs outside our inventory

Compare first checks our inventory records. An outside VIN may be decoded through NHTSA’s vPIC VIN decoder or matched to a supported original sticker. Decoded specifications do not establish that a vehicle is for sale, its advertised price, or every installed option. Treat a decoder-only record as specifications until a dealer listing or vehicle-specific document supports more detail.

How we match your wording to equipment

A shopper might say “air-conditioned seats” when a manufacturer calls the feature “ventilated seats.” We interpret supported everyday wording, then check evidence for the feature on the exact vehicle. Understanding the request does not create equipment that the source never verified. Exterior paint, interior color and wheel finish are checked separately; black wheels alone do not make a truck’s exterior black.

A trim or package name is a starting point. Optional equipment may replace standard equipment, and package contents can change by model year. Read our two-truck screen and equipment-group example and seat-feature guide for practical comparisons.

Why a field may say Verify or Unknown

Missing evidence is not proof that an option is absent. Unreadable labels, unsupported layouts and incomplete package information remain unconfirmed. Confirmed absence requires sufficient vehicle-specific evidence and applicable factory rules. Ask for a walkaround when a feature matters to your decision.

Editorial responsibility and corrections

Cars With Sam publishes these shopping guides. Sam owns the site and is the contact for questions and corrections.
