Jeep Grand Cherokee / L / 4xe: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Jeep Grand Cherokee / L / 4xe, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Grand Cherokee or Grand Cherokee L: which has three rows?

The standard Grand Cherokee has two rows and five seats. The longer Grand Cherokee L provides three rows and up to seven seats; check the actual second-row layout when matching your family’s needs.

Jeep Grand Cherokee FAQ ↗

What changed on the 2026 Grand Cherokee?

The refreshed 2026 range brings new exterior styling and a 12.3-inch touchscreen. Its new 2.0-liter Hurricane turbo engine is not on Laredo or Laredo X, which retain the 3.6-liter V6.

What mileage difference is there between the gas engines?

Jeep lists manufacturer estimates up to 21/27 city/highway mpg for the 2026 turbo four-cylinder and 19/26 for the V6. Compare the specific drivetrain and sticker; those maximum estimates are not promises for every configuration.

Jeep Grand Cherokee capability ↗

Is a trailer hitch enough to give it the maximum tow rating?

No. Jeep advertises up to 6,200 pounds for the 2026 gas models, but the vehicle’s configuration and required equipment establish its limit. Check the exact tow package before matching a loaded trailer.

Can I still buy a Grand Cherokee 4xe?

Jeep says Grand Cherokee 4xe production has ended, but remaining dealer stock and used vehicles may be available. Owner support continues; compare that specific plug-in hybrid’s service history and charging setup before choosing.

Jeep 4xe owner resources ↗

What does Quadra-Lift air suspension actually do?

Available Quadra-Lift changes ride height: it can lower the Grand Cherokee at highway speed and raise it for trail clearance. It is separate equipment, so confirm it rather than assuming every upscale trim has it.

Jeep Grand Cherokee capability ↗

Will child seats fit while people can still reach the third row?

Grand Cherokee models include LATCH systems, and the L adds a third row. Bring your child seats and test rear-seat access with them installed; seating capacity alone does not answer the practical fit question.

Jeep Grand Cherokee technology ↗

Is the surround-view camera the same as a backup camera?

No. The available 360-degree system provides views around the vehicle, while a backup camera covers the rear. Look specifically for Surround View Camera on the sticker and demonstrate its views in the vehicle.

Jeep Grand Cherokee technology ↗

Can it show me the children in the back seats?

Grand Cherokee L offers an available interior FamCAM for viewing rear passengers. It is different from the exterior surround-view system; confirm that the specific L has the interior camera you want.

Jeep Grand Cherokee technology ↗

Limited, Overland or Summit: which upgrades are worth paying for?

Compare the same model year and body style first. Prioritize the upgrades you need—such as adjustable air suspension, seat comfort or camera equipment—then compare the actual stickers. Pay for useful differences, not an assumption that every higher trim includes every option.
