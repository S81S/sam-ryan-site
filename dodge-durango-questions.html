Dodge Durango: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Dodge Durango, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Does this Durango seat six or seven people?

Durango can seat up to seven, but second-row seat configuration changes capacity and third-row access. Inspect the actual cabin and bring your child seats or regular passengers before deciding which layout works.

Durango FAQ ↗

Can it tow my boat or camper with everyone aboard?

Durango’s maximum can reach 8,700 pounds when properly equipped. Engine, package and vehicle ratings matter; include passengers, luggage and trailer tongue weight when checking the specific SUV against your fully loaded trailer.

Durango performance ↗

Does every HEMI Durango have the Tow ’n Go package?

No. The 2026 Tow ’n Go package adds performance brakes, adaptive suspension, a trailer brake controller and other hardware. A hitch alone is not equivalent. Consider the package for its towing and handling benefits, then compare the actual rating, price and replacement-tire costs.

Shopper discussion ↗Durango Tow ’n Go equipment ↗

Should I choose the V6, HEMI or Hellcat for daily use?

Compare your towing requirement and desired performance with fuel expense and driving feel. These engines have very different outputs and efficiency. Model-year lineups change, so identify the actual engine before comparing trim names.

Durango FAQ ↗

Does the Durango need premium gasoline?

Fuel guidance depends on engine: the V6 uses regular, the 5.7-liter HEMI recommends midgrade, and Hellcat requires premium. Follow the exact model-year manual rather than applying one fuel recommendation to every Durango.

Durango FAQ ↗

Is this one AWD, and can I tow it behind a motorhome?

AWD does not automatically mean flat-tow capability. The 2026 manual distinguishes drivetrain and transfer-case types and excludes some configurations. Match the exact vehicle to the recreational-towing instructions before choosing it for an RV.

2026 Durango owner manual ↗

How much luggage fits when the third row is being used?

Maximum cargo measurements use folded seats. For family travel, test the space behind the occupied third row with your stroller, bags or sports gear; seating capacity and practical luggage capacity must work together.

Durango design ↗

Does Blacktop mean better performance or mostly a different look?

Blacktop primarily changes appearance; Tow ’n Go and other equipment groups address different needs. Read each package’s model-year contents rather than assuming black wheels or exterior trim establish upgraded towing or performance hardware.

2026 Durango equipment guide ↗

Which package gets ventilated seats, better audio and the larger screen?

Compare these items individually on the sticker. Durango packages can combine comfort, infotainment and towing equipment, but their content varies by year and trim; a familiar package name is not a complete feature checklist.

2026 Durango equipment guide ↗

What should I check before buying a used performance Durango?

Review maintenance and inspect tires, brakes and any modifications, then check the VIN for open recalls. Ask for a vehicle inspection; an SRT badge or clean appearance does not establish current mechanical condition.
