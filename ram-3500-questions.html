Ram 3500 pickup: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Ram 3500 pickup, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Do I actually need a dually?

Start with loaded trailer pin weight and cargo, then compare single- and dual-rear-wheel configurations. A dually can provide the needed capacity, but its extra width also matters for your driveway and everyday parking.

Ram truck buying guide ↗

Can every Ram 3500 tow the advertised maximum?

No. The 2026 maximum of 36,610 pounds applies to a properly configured diesel truck. Engine, axle ratio, cab, rear wheels and equipment affect ratings; match your truck to the official configuration chart.

Ram 3500 capability ↗

How do I know my fifth-wheel pin weight will fit?

Add the loaded pin weight, hitch, occupants and truck cargo. Compare the total with payload and rear-axle limits. A truck can meet a trailer-towing figure yet exceed another rating when the trailer is attached.

Ram towing: before you buy ↗

Is diesel always the best choice for carrying heavy loads?

Not automatically. The 2026 gas lineup reaches a higher maximum payload, while diesel delivers the higher maximum towing rating. Compare the exact trucks with your body, camper or trailer load rather than engine torque alone.

Ram 3500 capability ↗

Should I choose Mega Cab or Crew Cab for family trips?

Compare usable rear-seat room and storage with the bed length you need. Cab choice also changes the truck’s overall size and configuration ratings, so passenger comfort and trailer clearance should be considered together.

Ram 3500 design ↗

Will a short bed work with my fifth-wheel trailer?

Compatibility depends on the specific trailer, hitch and cab clearance through turns. Factory prep alone does not resolve clearance. Have the complete combination checked before choosing a bed length for a fifth-wheel setup.

What does the factory fifth-wheel/gooseneck package provide?

It supplies integrated mounting hardware and a structural crossmember that can accept a suitable hitch. Confirm which hitch and electrical connections are present on the actual truck, especially when comparing used vehicles.

Ram 3500 capability ↗

Is the larger screen useful for towing, or just a luxury?

Screen size and towing functions are separate checks. The 2026 lineup offers a 14.5-inch center display, but identify the cameras, trailer features and controls you need rather than buying only by display size.

Ram 3500 overview ↗

Does this 3500 have the big fuel tank?

The 2026 lineup offers a 50-gallon tank on certain Crew Cab trucks with the 8-foot bed. Do not assume every diesel or long-bed truck includes it; check the factory equipment for that VIN.

Ram 3500 capability ↗

What should I check on a used truck that has worked hard?

Review maintenance, tires, suspension, driveline and hitch condition, then arrange an inspection matched to its intended workload. Ask about modifications and compare installed components with the factory specifications before accepting its advertised capability.
