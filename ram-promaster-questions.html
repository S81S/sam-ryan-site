Ram ProMaster: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Ram ProMaster, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Which roof height lets me stand up and still fit where I park?

The 2026 cargo heights are 66, 77 or 86 inches before an upfit. Added floors and ceilings reduce usable headroom; roof racks increase exterior height. Measure both your finished interior and parking clearance.

Which wheelbase and cargo length do I need?

Choose around the longest items you carry and the room needed to work around them. ProMaster offers several cargo lengths; the biggest volume figure belongs to a specific configuration, not every high-roof van.

Will pallets and bulky equipment fit through the doors?

Check the opening as well as interior space. The 2026 passenger-side opening is listed at 49 inches and the rear opening at 60 inches; your upfit, partition or loading equipment can reduce practical access.

How much can I load after shelves or a camper conversion?

Shelving, batteries, water, passengers and equipment all use payload. Start with the actual van’s weight ratings and weigh the completed build; the lineup’s advertised maximum does not describe every roof and wheelbase combination.

Is ProMaster front-wheel drive or all-wheel drive?

The gas ProMaster uses front-wheel drive. If all-wheel drive is essential to your work, do not assume a cargo-van listing provides it; assess tire choice, road conditions and the actual drivetrain before committing.

Can I tow a trailer while carrying my normal work load?

The 2026 maximum towing figure is 6,910 pounds when properly equipped, with lower ratings on some configurations. Account for cargo and tongue weight, and confirm the exact van, hitch and combined-weight limits together.

Will the long van be awkward in tight loading areas?

Try the exact wheelbase on your normal parking and loading route. ProMaster’s best turning-diameter figure does not describe every body length; rear overhang and door clearance also matter when maneuvering around obstacles.

Which cameras and driver aids help when rear visibility is blocked?

Check the installed camera and mirror systems with the cargo partition or finished body in place. Available driver assistance and display choices differ, so demonstrate the views and warnings that matter on your routes.

Does Active Driving Assist drive the van for me?

No. The available system can assist speed, following distance and lane positioning within its operating limits. You remain responsible, attentive and hands-on; evaluate the installed system rather than assuming all ProMaster vans include it.

What maintenance costs should I plan for on a working van?

Match servicing to the van’s actual use and manufacturer instructions, including mileage, time and operating conditions. Ask for records on a used van and include tires, brakes and upfit maintenance when comparing ownership costs.
