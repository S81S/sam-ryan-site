Chrysler Voyager: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Chrysler Voyager, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

What do I give up by choosing Voyager over Pacifica?

Voyager emphasizes a simpler, value-oriented configuration. Pacifica offers additional drivetrain, seating and premium-equipment choices. Compare the exact vans against your essentials instead of assuming every feature difference matters to your family.

Voyager FAQ ↗

Can I get a Voyager with AWD?

The current Voyager is front-wheel drive. For AWD, consider an equipped gas Pacifica. The vans share a family resemblance but offer different drivetrain choices.

Voyager FAQ ↗

Does the Voyager seat seven or eight people?

The current Voyager seats up to seven. Check the actual seating layout and child-seat access during your visit; if eight seats are essential, look at a Pacifica configuration that specifically provides them.

Voyager FAQ ↗

Does it have second- and third-row Stow ’n Go?

The current Voyager is described with both rows of Stow ’n Go. When shopping older used examples, verify their actual seat configuration and demonstrate the folding sequence so missing seats or equipment differences are caught.

Voyager FAQ ↗

How much can the Voyager tow?

Chrysler lists 1,500 pounds when equipped for trailer towing. That differs from a properly equipped Pacifica’s higher maximum. Match the actual van’s manual, hitch and loaded trailer before treating the two models as interchangeable.

Voyager FAQ ↗

What fuel economy should I expect?

The current Voyager’s 3.6-liter V6 has an advertised highway rating up to 28 mpg. Highway ratings are not city-driving promises; compare your route, passenger load and fuel budget with the specific vehicle’s EPA label.

Voyager capability ↗

Does Voyager have a small screen or the 10.1-inch system?

The current Voyager features a 10.1-inch Uconnect 5 center touchscreen and a separate driver display. Older examples may differ, so identify the model year and actual radio rather than relying on a generic listing photo.

Voyager technology ↗

Can I connect my phone without a cable?

The current Voyager supports wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Test your compatible phone during the visit, and check older vehicles individually; Bluetooth audio alone is not the same as full smartphone projection.

Voyager technology ↗

Does it include adaptive cruise and blind-spot monitoring?

Chrysler highlights these features on the current Voyager. Confirm the exact year and installed equipment on used vans, then demonstrate the controls; assistance features supplement attentive driving and should not be confused with self-driving capability.

Voyager technology ↗

Will it have enough passing power with the family aboard?

The current Voyager uses a 287-horsepower V6. A test drive with your normal passenger and cargo needs is more useful than the number alone; evaluate acceleration, visibility and comfort together before deciding.
