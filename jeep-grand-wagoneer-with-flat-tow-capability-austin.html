Flat towing: what to know

Flat towing means towing the vehicle behind a motorhome with all four wheels on the ground. For the vehicles below, the factory owner’s manual supports recreational flat towing for that drivetrain.

Follow the complete procedure in the owner’s manual for the specific vehicle, and confirm the towing equipment and the vehicle’s condition before towing.
