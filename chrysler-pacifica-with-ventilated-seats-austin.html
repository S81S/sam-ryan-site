Ventilated seats: what to know

Ventilated seats move air through the seat surface to keep you cooler. Shoppers often call them cooled or air-conditioned seats. They are frequently part of an equipment group, so a trim name alone does not confirm them. Ventilated, heated and massaging seats explained.
