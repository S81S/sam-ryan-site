Get specific about your truck

Type your requirements in your own words, such as “Ram 1500 under $60,000 with ventilated seats.” Add the features you want to the same search. Model year, engine, trim and option packages can change what is fitted, so a trim name alone is not confirmation of every feature.

For example, cooled seats and ventilated seats may describe the same feature in a shopper’s request. Massaging seats are a separate feature. Understand the difference before you compare.

Compare two trucks by stock number

Found two Ram 1500 trucks you like? Enter their stock numbers in Compare to see equipment differences together. Original window stickers matched to each VIN provide the factory-equipment evidence when available. An unavailable sticker or incomplete information does not mean a truck lacks a feature.

For towing, tell us what you plan to tow and ask us to confirm the specific truck’s ratings and configuration. Do not choose a truck’s towing capability from its model name alone.
