Dodge Challenger · used: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Dodge Challenger, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Should I choose a V6, R/T, Scat Pack or Hellcat?

Begin with desired driving feel and running costs, then identify the actual engine. Challenger trims span V6, naturally aspirated V8 and supercharged V8 powertrains; badges and appearance changes are not reliable substitutes for factory specifications.

2023 Challenger factory specifications ↗

Can I get a Challenger with AWD and a V8?

In the 2023 factory lineup, AWD belongs to the V6 SXT and GT configurations; V8 models use rear-wheel drive. Check the specific year when shopping used, and do not mistake an AWD GT for a V8.

Which Challengers have a manual transmission?

Manual availability depends on engine, trim and year. The 2023 lineup includes certain V8 manual configurations, while other versions use automatics. Verify the actual car and transmission rather than assuming all R/T or Hellcat listings match.

2023 Challenger factory specifications ↗

Does every Challenger require premium fuel?

No. For 2023, the V6 uses regular; the automatic 5.7-liter recommends midgrade, while the manual 5.7-liter requires premium. Other performance engines have their own requirements, so use the correct engine and transmission instructions.

2023 Challenger owner manual ↗

Will the back seat work for my passengers?

Most road-going Challenger configurations provide five-passenger seating, but access is through two doors. Try your actual child seats or rear passengers; nominal seat count cannot tell you how comfortable entry and everyday travel will be.

Does it have CarPlay, navigation and the bigger touchscreen?

Those are separate infotainment checks. A supported phone connection does not prove built-in navigation or the larger screen. Compare the actual radio description and demonstrate your phone in the car, especially on older model years.

2023 Challenger interior ↗

Are heated and ventilated seats included with leather?

Do not assume so from upholstery alone. Check the exact seat and package equipment, and operate both heating and ventilation during the test drive; comfort options differ across Challenger years and configurations.

2023 Challenger interior ↗

Is a used Challenger with modifications a better deal?

Ask what changed, who installed it and whether supporting records exist. Have the engine, transmission, brakes and tires inspected for the intended use; added performance parts are not proof of condition or trouble-free ownership.

Challenger maintenance schedule ↗

What maintenance should I budget for besides oil changes?

Use the correct engine and transmission schedule, including mileage, age and demanding-use conditions. Tires, brakes and driveline servicing deserve particular attention on a performance car; compare records with the maintenance actually due.

Challenger maintenance schedule ↗

Can I still buy parts and get service now that production ended?

Yes. Dodge directs Challenger owners to its dealers and Mopar for available parts and service. Production ended in 2023, so focus on the individual used car’s condition, history and remaining coverage when comparing examples.
