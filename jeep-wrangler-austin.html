Which Wrangler roof fits your routine?

Jeep lists several roof choices, including the Sunrider soft top, Freedom Top three-piece hardtop and Sky One-Touch power top. You may also see Sunrider for Hardtop, which is a distinct option to check rather than assuming it means the vehicle has a full soft top. See Jeep’s roof options.

  • Soft top: Ask for a demonstration of opening and closing the fitted top. On a used Wrangler, inspect the fabric, windows and fasteners, and confirm which pieces are included.
  • Three-piece hardtop: Talk through which panels you plan to remove and where you would store them. Ask to see the actual roof and included hardware before deciding.
  • Sky One-Touch power top: Ask to see the fitted power top operate and compare that experience with manually opening a top. Confirm the exact option on the vehicle’s VIN-matched window sticker.

Roof availability depends on model year and configuration. A trim name or a stock photo does not establish the roof on a particular Wrangler. Check the factory window sticker against current photos and a walkaround; a used vehicle may have a different top or added accessories today.

Two doors or four?

Wrangler comes in two-door and four-door body styles. Before picking one, think about how often passengers use the rear seat and what you carry each week. Try rear-seat access and bring the items you need to fit in the cargo area. Jeep’s Wrangler FAQ provides current model information; verify dimensions and seating for the year and configuration you are considering.

For an Austin test drive, include the kinds of roads you use most. Listen with the roof closed, check your view from the driver’s seat and try parking. If the vehicle has different tires, a lift or other modifications, ask what has changed from its factory configuration.
