Jeep Wagoneer S: 10 questions worth asking before you choose

Published by Cars With Sam, the shopping site created and owned by Samuel “Sam” Sweitzer. Researched October 4, 2026.

Selected buying questions about the Jeep Wagoneer S, answered from manufacturer documents, owner information and real shopper discussions. Equipment changes by model year and package, so check the exact vehicle with Sam or Ryan.

Is Wagoneer S electric, hybrid or gas?

The 2025 Wagoneer S is fully electric. It requires charging and has no gasoline backup, so evaluate home charging and your usual travel routes before comparing it with the larger gasoline Wagoneer family.

Jeep Wagoneer S overview ↗

How far will it go between charges?

Jeep lists up to 294 miles for the 2025 Launch Edition under its stated test conditions. Weather, speed, terrain and equipment can change actual range; plan trips around charging stops rather than the maximum alone.

Jeep Wagoneer S overview ↗

How long does a quick highway charging stop take?

Jeep advertises an estimated 23 minutes from 20% to 80% using a suitable DC fast charger. Charging time varies with conditions and the station; that figure is not a zero-to-full charge.

Jeep Wagoneer S overview ↗

Can I charge it at home overnight?

A professionally installed 240-volt Level 2 station is the faster home option. Jeep’s 2025 FAQ quotes approximately 6.8 hours under its stated conditions; a normal 120-volt outlet takes much longer.

Jeep Wagoneer S FAQ ↗

Does Wagoneer S have a third row?

No. The 2025 Wagoneer S seats five. For three rows, compare Grand Cherokee L or Grand Wagoneer; the Wagoneer name does not guarantee the same cabin.

Jeep Wagoneer S FAQ ↗

How much luggage space does the Wagoneer S have?

Jeep lists 30.6 cubic feet behind the rear seats and 61 with them folded for 2025, plus a small front compartment. Test your usual luggage with passengers’ seats in their normal positions.

Jeep Wagoneer S FAQ ↗

What do I get with Launch Edition over Limited?

The 2025 FAQ lists 500 horsepower for Limited and available 600 horsepower for Launch Edition. Seat adjustment and comfort equipment also differ; compare the exact sticker instead of treating every Wagoneer S as identical.

Jeep Wagoneer S FAQ ↗

Can I get ventilated seats and premium audio?

The 2025 Wagoneer S offers ventilated-seat equipment and available McIntosh audio. Compare front and rear seat features and the installed audio system separately; those details are more useful than a seller’s “fully loaded” description.

Jeep Wagoneer S FAQ ↗

Should I charge to 100% every day?

The 2025 owner manual provides selectable maximum-charge settings, including 80% Daily Range and 100% Full Range. Choose the setting appropriate to your use and follow the manual’s battery-care and charging guidance.

2025 Wagoneer S owner manual ↗

What should I check on a used Wagoneer S?

Review its remaining warranty, service history and charging equipment, and run the exact VIN through NHTSA for unrepaired safety recalls. Ask to demonstrate charging; a displayed range estimate alone does not establish battery condition.
