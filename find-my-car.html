How your equipment search works

Does this search read window stickers?

Yes. The saved equipment index uses readable original window stickers matched to each vehicle’s VIN. Search results show factory wording and link to the source. Some vehicles have no readable sticker available, so their equipment remains unverified.

Can I search for a HEMI V8, cooled seats and a sunroof?

Yes. Try “Ram 1500 HEMI V8 under $60k with cooled seats and a sunroof.” Each requested feature must be supported by the VIN-matched sticker to count as a confirmed match. Cooled or air-conditioned seat requests are mapped to ventilated/cooled seats, with the factory wording shown.

How does “no sunroof” work?

A sticker must explicitly exclude the feature, such as a sunroof deletion, for a confirmed negative match. A missing mention stays unknown. This avoids telling you a vehicle lacks a feature when the sticker does not prove it.

Are sticker prices and equipment current?

A factory window sticker describes original equipment and MSRP. Search budgets use the saved dealership asking price, and mileage comes from the listing. Confirm today’s availability, final price, installed equipment and condition before visiting.
